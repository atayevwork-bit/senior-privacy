SeniorAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

SeniorAI ("the app") is an AI chat assistant that runs on your phone. It was developed by Atayev Dayanch ("the developer", "we"). This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: we do not run any servers that receive your data, we do not collect personal information, and your conversations stay on your device.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information on our servers. The app has no accounts, no sign-up and no login. It does not contain advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

2. Conversations and settings (stored on your device only)

Your chat history and app settings (such as light/dark mode) are saved in the app's private storage on your phone so you can reopen earlier chats. This data is never sent to us. You can delete individual chats inside the app, or remove all app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

3. How the AI works

The app uses a language model that runs locally on your device. Your messages are processed on your phone to generate answers and are not sent to us or to any server for this purpose.

4. Model download

When you first set up the app, it downloads its language model (about 2.6 GB) over the internet from a public file host (Hugging Face). To deliver the file, that host receives standard connection information such as your IP address and device/network details, as with any file download. We do not receive this information, and no chat content is included in the download. Hugging Face's own privacy policy applies to that connection. After the download is complete, the app can be used without an internet connection.

5. Voice input (optional)

If speech recognition is available on your device, the app shows a microphone button. When you tap it, the app asks your device's built-in speech recognition service to convert your speech into text. That service is provided by your device or operating system, not by us, and it may process audio according to its provider's own privacy policy and your device settings. The app does not record or store audio itself, and it only receives the resulting text. Voice input is optional; you can type instead. The app requests no microphone permission of its own and only uses the system speech dialog.

6. Permissions

  • Internet: used only to download the language model.
  • Storage: the model and your chats are stored in the app's private storage; the app does not read your photos, contacts, location or other files.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not collect it.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. AI-generated content

Answers are generated by an AI model and may be inaccurate or incomplete. Please verify important information and do not rely on the app for medical, legal or financial decisions.

10. Your choices and data deletion

Because your data stays on your device, you control it. You can delete chats in the app, clear the app's storage in your phone settings, or uninstall the app to remove all data it stored, including the downloaded model.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
